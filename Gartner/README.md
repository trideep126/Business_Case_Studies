
## **Project Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing market share trends, vendor revenue distributions, and mergers & acquisitions using Power BI. The data is structured across Silver and Gold layers for efficient reporting and insights generation.

## **Project Goal**

To provide a comprehensive and visually intuitive dashboard that enables stakeholders to understand market trends, vendor performance, and the impact of mergers & acquisitions, aiding in data-driven decision-making.

## **Project Requirements**

- Structured data across Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers.
- Power BI dashboard with key visualizations for market analysis.
- Insights into vendor market share, revenue distribution, and M&A activities.
- Efficient data transformation and aggregation for real-time reporting.

## **Design Architecture**

1. **Bronze Layer**: Raw data ingestion.
2. **Silver Layer**: Cleaned and structured data with vendor-specific details.
3. **Gold Layer**: Aggregated analytics for reporting.
4. **Power BI Dashboard**: Interactive visualizations for stakeholder insights.
