# Data Catalog

## Overview
This document provides an overview of the datasets used in the Power BI dashboard project. It includes details about the schema, source, and purpose of each table.

## **1. silver.market_share**
**Description:** Contains market share data of various vendors over time.
**Columns:**
- `vendor` – Name of the vendor
- `vendor_company` – Parent company of the vendor (if applicable)
- `mkt_year` – Year of the market share data
- `vendor_revenue` – Revenue generated by the vendor
- `super_region` – High-level geographic classification
- `region` – Region of operation
- `country` – Country of operation
- `service_1`, `service_2`, `service_3` – Different service categories
- `vertical` – Industry vertical
- `ticker` – Stock ticker symbol (if applicable)
- `hq_cntry` – Country of headquarters
- `cc_revenue` – Revenue contribution from specific business units

## **2. silver.vendor_mergers**
**Description:** Tracks mergers and acquisitions in the market.
**Columns:**
- `vendor` – Vendor involved in the merger
- `acquiring_company` – The company acquiring the vendor
- `acquired_company` – The company being acquired
- `mkt_year` – Year in which the acquisition occurred

## **3. gold.market_share_analytics**
**Description:** A refined dataset for market share analysis with rankings and revenue breakdowns.
**Columns:**
- `mkt_year` – Year of analysis
- `region` – Region of operation
- `country` – Country of operation
- `vendor` – Vendor name
- `total_revenue` – Total revenue generated by the vendor
- `market_share_percentage` – Market share held by the vendor in the region
- `ranking` – Vendor ranking based on market share

## **Usage in Power BI**
- **Market Share Trends:** Uses `gold.market_share_analytics` for historical trends.
- **Region-wise Vendor Revenue:** Uses `silver.market_share` for geographical revenue distribution.
- **Top Vendors & Market Revenue:** Derived from `gold.market_share_analytics`.
- **Mergers & Acquisitions Tracking:** Uses `silver.vendor_mergers` to display market consolidations.

## **Updates & Maintenance**
- Data is refreshed periodically based on updated market reports.
- Ensure consistency in `vendor` and `company` names across tables.
- Regular validation is required to maintain data integrity.
